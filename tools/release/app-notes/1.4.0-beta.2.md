The second beta of 1.4: two more kinds of robot, a shape for every machine, and a simpler tab bar on phones.

## Robots

- **Two-wheeled balancing robots.** A ground robot shape that leans into what it does: forward to speed up, back to brake. Try the simulated one, Wobble.
- **Humanoids.** A legged robot shape on two legs, with knees and elbows: it walks, steps sideways, turns, crouches with the height stick, and stands or sits. Try the simulated one, Ada.
- **Pick a shape.** Devices > Shape chooses what the 3D view draws for a device whose firmware does not say (a hexacopter, a tracked rover, a catamaran, a humanoid…). Auto keeps the one it declares.

## Guides

- **Legged robot.** A new device example: the sticks and commands, standing and sitting, gaits, and a complete program for Arduino, Python and MicroPython. In every language.

## Phones

- **Four tabs.** Live, Control, Automate and More; More opens Tune, Replay, Devices, Guides and Settings.

## Fixes

- The top bar keeps STOP and its controls in view with two devices and an alert, in every language, and its link, battery and status sit centred.
