A better fit for phones, held either way.

- **Held sideways:** Control is laid out like a game controller, with bigger sticks in the bottom corners, the readouts between them and the buttons in two compact rows at the top. Live gets its own layout too, with taller tiles in the corners and the 3D view in the middle. Layouts you have already edited stay as they are.
- **iPhone, sideways:** the screens use the whole height down to the bottom edge. On Live and Control the home indicator dims, and a first swipe up from the edge stays in the app, so a stick moved near the edge does not send you home; swipe again to leave.
- **iPhone, upright:** the tab bar is slimmer and reaches into the strip at the bottom, leaving only the part the home swipe needs.
