The first release of koebelt dash, the companion app for drones, rovers, robot arms, rockets and test benches. Pair it with the koebelt/link library (Arduino, Python, Rust, ROS 2), or connect an ArduPilot, PX4, Betaflight or INAV board, or a DJI Tello, as it is.

## What it does

- **Live**: HUD over a 3D scene, a map or the camera, with a widget grid per device and a wall of sensor tiles.
- **Control**: sticks, sliders or phone tilt for drones and rovers; jog and gripper for arms; arm, countdown and abort for rockets; throttle and sequences for test benches. Game controllers and RC transmitters work too.
- **Program**: step sequences per device, stored on the device so they carry on if the link drops.
- **Tune**: the parameters your firmware declares, with a live response chart.
- **Several devices at once**, up to 8, with STOP stopping them all.
- **Replay**: record sessions with the camera and every connected device, replay them, export CSV and video, import ArduPilot and PX4 logs.
- **Links**: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth LE, USB serial, SiK/XBee/HC-12/LoRa radios and Bluetooth serial modules.
- **Simulated devices** to try everything without hardware.

The failsafe runs on the device, which holds, returns or lands on its own when the app goes quiet. Still: test with props off first.
