koebelt dash now speaks your language and your units, and knows more machines: planes, boats, legged robots, two-wheeled balancing robots and humanoids, each with its own 3D shape.

## Languages

- **Seven languages.** English, French, Spanish, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, guides included. Settings > Language follows the system, or picks one.
- **Made with AI.** Every translation but English was made with AI and may contain mistakes; a note in Settings and Guides says so and links to this repository, where corrections are welcome.
- **Traditional Chinese.** A system in Traditional Chinese is asked once, at first launch, whether to use Simplified Chinese, English or another language.

## Units

- **Metric or imperial.** Settings > Units shows lengths in feet, speeds in mph and temperatures in °F, or follows the region (imperial in the US). Readouts, charts, alerts, sequences, the map and the 3D view all follow; devices, scripts and recordings keep metres.

## More machines

- **Planes.** A fixed-wing plane takes off, flies a circuit through waypoints, circles while it holds and lands in a glide. Try the simulated one, Swift.
- **Boats.** Driven like a ground robot, drifting with the current. Try Otter.
- **Legged robots.** Quadrupeds, hexapods and humanoids walk forward, sideways and turning at once: the right stick walks, the left one turns and sets the body height. Stand and sit, walk and trot from Control; sequences walk to waypoints. Try Scout (a quadruped) and Ada (a humanoid).
- **Two-wheeled balancing robots.** A ground robot that leans into what it does. Try Wobble.
- **Shapes.** The 3D view draws what your machine looks like: a quad, hexa, octo or helicopter; a trainer, flying wing or VTOL plane; a 4WD, car, tracked or balancing rover; a hull or a catamaran; a quadruped, hexapod or humanoid. Devices declare it, ArduPilot and PX4 vehicles get it from their frame type, and Devices > Shape picks it for firmware that does not say.
- **ArduPilot and PX4.** Planes, quadplanes and boats connect as such, and ArduPlane and PX4 fixed-wing logs open as planes.

## koebelt/link 1.2.0

- The Arduino, Python and Rust libraries and the ROS 2 bridge declare planes, boats and legged robots, and their shape, and run their stored sequences.
- **MicroPython.** A new library for an ESP32 or a Raspberry Pi Pico W, over Wi-Fi, USB, a UART, a radio or a Bluetooth serial module: `mpremote mip install github:koebelt/koebelt_dash/libs/micropython`. Guides > Quickstart > MicroPython.
- New device guide: Legged robot, with a complete program for Arduino, Python and MicroPython.

## Also

- **Linux on ARM64** (Raspberry Pi 5, Jetson, ARM laptops), next to x64; updates pick the right one. Chinese and Japanese use the system's CJK font: `install.sh` says when to add `fonts-noto-cjk`.
- **Phones: four tabs.** Live, Control, Automate and More; More opens Tune, Replay, Devices, Guides and Settings.
- **The 3D view** shows the ground as far as the camera sees, its lines fading with distance; simulated planes bank into their turns.
- Long words in every language fit: buttons, tabs, readouts and the top bar, at every screen size.
- `koebelt-dash-scripts` 1.4.0 is on PyPI.

## Updating

From 1.1.0 or later, use the Update button in the app. From 1.0.0, install this version by hand once.
