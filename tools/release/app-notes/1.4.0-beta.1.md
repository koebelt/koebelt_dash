The first beta of 1.4: koebelt dash in your language, in your units, for more machines and more boards.

## Languages

- **Seven languages.** English, French, Spanish, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Japanese and Simplified Chinese, guides included. Settings > Language follows the system, or picks one.
- **Made with AI.** Every translation but English was made with AI and may contain mistakes; a note in Settings and Guides says so and links to the repository, where corrections are welcome.
- **Traditional Chinese.** A system in Traditional Chinese is asked once, at first launch, whether to use Simplified Chinese, English or another language.

## Units

- **Metric or imperial.** Settings > Units shows lengths in feet, speeds in mph and temperatures in °F, or follows the region (imperial in the US). Readouts, charts, alerts, sequences, the map and the 3D view all follow; devices, scripts and recordings keep metres.

## Planes and boats

- **Two new device kinds.** A fixed-wing plane takes off, flies a circuit through waypoints, circles while it holds and lands in a glide; a boat is driven like a ground robot and drifts with the current. Both have their own 3D model, dashboard and sequences, and a simulated one to try (Swift and Otter).
- **ArduPilot and PX4.** Planes, quadplanes and boats connect as such, and ArduPlane and PX4 fixed-wing logs open as planes.
- **koebelt/link.** The Arduino, Python and Rust libraries declare planes and boats (`KLink::PLANE`, `kind="boat"`, `Kind::Plane`), and run their stored sequences.

## Legged robots and body shapes

- **Legged robots.** Quadrupeds and hexapods walk forward, sideways and turning at once: the right stick walks, the left one turns and sets the body height. Stand and sit, walk and trot from Control; sequences walk to waypoints. A simulated one (Scout) stands up when armed.
- **Body shapes.** The 3D view draws what your machine looks like: a quad, hexa, octo or helicopter; a trainer, flying wing or VTOL plane; a 4WD, car or tracked rover; a hull or a catamaran; a quadruped or a hexapod. ArduPilot and PX4 vehicles get theirs from their frame type; koebelt/link devices declare it (`body="hexa"`, `setBody("car")`, `.body("tracked")`).

## 3D view

- The ground grid reaches as far as the camera sees, and its lines fade in and out with distance instead of popping.
- Simulated planes bank into their turns, and drones lean into their slide.

## MicroPython

- **koebelt/link for MicroPython.** One file for an ESP32 or a Raspberry Pi Pico W, over Wi-Fi, USB, a UART, a radio or a Bluetooth serial module: `mpremote mip install github:koebelt/koebelt_dash/libs/micropython`. Guides > Quickstart > MicroPython, and a complete ground robot in the device examples.

## Linux

- **ARM64.** A Linux build for ARM64 (Raspberry Pi 5, Jetson, ARM laptops), next to the x64 one; updates pick the right one.
- **Chinese and Japanese** use the system's CJK font: `install.sh` says when to add `fonts-noto-cjk`.

## Fixes

- Many layouts checked at every screen size and in every language: long words no longer overflow buttons, tabs and readouts.
