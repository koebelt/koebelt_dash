- Automate: one page for the two ways to automate a device, Sequences (steps in order, no code) and Scripts (Python), with a guide to choose.
- Scripts: drive your devices from Python with any package (OpenCV, PyTorch, YOLO). Write, check and edit them in the app; they run on a computer on your network, paired once as a script host, while your iPhone or iPad keeps the device link, the sticks and STOP.
- Pictures and charts from your scripts show in Live, and are kept with the recording.
- Triggers: fire a script's actions from buttons, keys, game-controller buttons, conditions like a battery below 25 % for 3 s, device events or timers.
- You stay in charge: STOP ends every script, moving the sticks takes over, and each script has limits you set.
- Modes, simpler: a device connects disarmed in HOLD, arming hands it to you in MANUAL, and AUTO only while a sequence or a script drives. The mode is shown and chosen on Live.
- Proximity sensors show on Live, with an alert when an obstacle is close.
- AI assistants: turn on Settings > AI assistants to let Claude or another MCP client on your computer read the guides, write scripts and use the app. Actions on real hardware wait for your approval.
- Maps from Mapbox: Dark, Outdoors and Satellite.
- Every screen fits: small iPhones, iPhone held sideways, iPad upright, and large text.
