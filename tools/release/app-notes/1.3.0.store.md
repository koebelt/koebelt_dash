- Scripts: drive your devices from Python with any package (OpenCV, PyTorch, YOLO), from a new Scripts screen under Program. Write and edit them in the app; they run on a computer on your network, paired once as a script host, while your iPhone or iPad keeps the device link, the sticks and STOP.
- Triggers: fire a script's actions from buttons, keys, game-controller buttons, conditions like battery.pct < 25 for 3s, device events or timers.
- You stay in charge: STOP ends every script, moving the sticks takes over, and each script has limits you set.
- AI assistants: turn on Settings > AI assistants to let Claude or another MCP client on your computer read the guides, write scripts and use the app. Actions on real hardware wait for your approval.
- New guides: Scripts, and AI assistants.
